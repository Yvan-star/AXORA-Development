## Stages

- **Idea:** problem and intended users are being explored.
- **Discovery:** evidence and requirements are being gathered.
- **Prototype:** a narrow end-to-end concept is being built.
- **Beta:** real users test a limited, supported release.
- **Production:** operational, security, support, and recovery practices are in place.
- **Paused / Retired:** inactive or no longer maintained.

Keep this registry factual. Do not label a product production-ready merely because a prototype runs.
